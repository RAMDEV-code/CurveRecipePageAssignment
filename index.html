Simple Omelette Recipe

An easy and quick dish, perfect for any meal. This classic omelette combines beaten eggs cooked to perfection, with optional cheese, vegetables, or herbs.

  • Ingredients
  • Instructions
  • Nutrition

Preparation Time

  • Total: Approximately 10 minutes
  • Preparation: 5 minutes
  • Cooking: 5 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2–3 large eggs
  • Salt, to taste
  • Pepper, to taste
  • 1 tablespoon of butter or oil
  • Optional fillings: cheese, diced vegetables, or herbs

Instructions

  1. Beat the eggs: Beat the eggs with salt and pepper until well mixed. Add a tablespoon of milk or water if desired.
  2. Heat the pan: Place a non-stick frying pan over medium heat and add butter or oil.
  3. Cook the omelette: Pour in the eggs once the butter has melted. Tilt the pan so the eggs spread evenly.
  4. Add fillings: When the edges begin to set, add your chosen fillings to one half of the omelette.
  5. Fold and serve: Fold the omelette in half and cook until the eggs are fully set.
  6. Enjoy: Serve hot and season to taste.
